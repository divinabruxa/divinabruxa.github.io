Take the Orb with you

Installation creates a Home Screen icon and opens Divina Bruxa like an app. It does not publish anything to an app store or change your account.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Confirm with “Add”.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Confirm installation.

Prepare the offline core

Prepares the interface, Free Tarot, an already revealed Daily Card, Library, local Journal and Local Whit without an API. Your current skin remains available. School and Premium spreads require a confirmed active entitlement; Account, purchases, billing, Admin, online Whit and Consultation submission still require a secure connection.

If an update is incomplete, verify or repair only the app's public files. Journal, Account and other private data are not deleted.
