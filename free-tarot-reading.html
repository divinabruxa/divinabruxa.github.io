How the free experience works

  1. Prepare the question

    Choose a question you can examine without asking the cards to replace facts or professional advice.

  2. Touch the Orb

    Each touch takes the next card from a freshly shuffled canonical deck. Revealed positions stay fixed.

  3. Observe before interpreting

    Notice image, sequence and response. Use the Library separately when you intentionally want editorial context.
