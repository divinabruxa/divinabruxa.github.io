Investment and partnerships

Divina Bruxa is a Brazilian digital tarot platform created by Hércules Dx. It brings together a 78-card deck, a Tarot School, and Whit, a digital tarot reader, in a space for practice, study, and reflection.

Explore the platform

Discover Free Tarot, Card of the Day, and the Orb of Realities that connects the site's experiences.

Where we want to grow

We aim to expand the Tarot School, improve the mobile experience, and bring the platform to new audiences in more languages.

Work with us

We welcome conversations with investors and partners in product development, digital education, technology, and distribution, in Brazil and internationally.
