LEARNING METHOD

A path from observation to synthesis

◇

Observe before naming

Begin with image, position and question before reaching for a fixed definition.

✦

Practice in context

Compare what a symbol suggests with facts, boundaries and choices that actually exist.

☾

Record your reasoning

A private notebook can reveal patterns in your interpretation without turning them into diagnosis.

☉

Review with ethics

Revise language that creates fear, certainty or dependence; return agency to the reader.

STARTER PRACTICE

A four-step study session

  1. Choose one upright card

    Write three details you can see without interpreting them.

  2. Name a possible dynamic

    Use conditional language: “this may suggest…” or “one possibility is…”.

  3. Connect it to context

    Separate facts, feelings, hypotheses and choices.

  4. Close with agency

    Write one question or small action that remains under your control.
